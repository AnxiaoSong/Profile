2019年9月起在西安电子科技大学进行硕博连读，师从沈玉龙教授，并于2026年获计算机科学与技术专业博士学位。期间，他于2024年至2025年赴奥克兰大学访学，合作导师为Giovanni Russello教授。他的本科毕业于河南工业大学软件工程专业。他将于2026年8月加入澳门城市大学担任教职。他的研究方向涵盖安全多方计算（MPC）、联邦学习、隐私保护机器学习、大小模型协同安全以及应用密码学。
>
> Anxiao Song is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology at Xidian University under Prof. Yulong Shen, and was a visiting researcher at the University of Auckland (2024–2025) under Prof. Giovanni Russello, funded by CSC. He received his B.E. from Henan University of Technology. He will join City University of Macau as a faculty member in August 2026. His research interests include secure MPC, federated learning, privacy-preserving ML, collaborative security for small and large models, and applied cryptography.
