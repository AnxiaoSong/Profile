    Anxiao Song is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology at Xidian University under Prof. Yulong Shen. He was a visiting researcher at the University of Auckland (2024–2025) under Prof. Giovanni Russello, funded by the China Scholarship Council. He received his B.E. in Software Engineering from Henan University of Technology. He will join the City University of Macau as a faculty member in August 2026. His research interests include secure multiparty computation (MPC), federated learning, privacy-preserving machine learning, collaborative security for small and large models, and applied cryptography.
